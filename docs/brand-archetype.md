# Brand Archetype

Zentrik Open CRM uses **The Signal Workshop** as its public brand archetype.

Open CRM should feel like a practical workshop where account signal, user
feedback, market evidence, agent work, human review, releases, and outcomes stay
connected. It is open enough to inspect, structured enough for agents, and calm
enough for real daily account work.

## What It Should Communicate

- this is a real CRM, not a marketing demo
- recommendations point back to source evidence
- users can shape what the product becomes
- agents prepare work, but humans keep review gates visible
- the product shows what changed, why, and whether it helped

## Voice

Use direct language:

- "built with its users"
- "signals become product work"
- "source-grounded account work"
- "human-reviewed agent work"
- "watch the product evolve"
- "show why it changed"

Avoid language that implies unchecked autonomy or AI magic:

- "self-driving CRM"
- "AI does your sales"
- "agentic magic"
- "autonomous CRM"

## Visual System

Use real product screenshots for product proof. Use generated imagery only as an
abstract backdrop for the signal-to-work loop.

The core visual motif is:

`feedback -> evidence -> agent work -> review -> release -> outcome`

Keep the visual style practical and source-grounded:

- workbench over spaceship
- receipts over vibes
- visible evolution over generic dashboards
- restrained blue, teal, amber, and green status colors
- deterministic text, never generated text
