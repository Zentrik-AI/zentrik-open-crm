# Product intent

Zentrik Open CRM is an open-source, local-first CRM for teams that want account
memory, source-grounded next actions, and agent assistance without giving up
control of their data.

It also provides a clear example of a human-and-agent product loop: users can
review what an agent proposes, keep the evidence behind a decision, and share
public-safe feedback that can inform future work.

## Target Users

- founders doing founder-led sales
- builders running small B2B products
- consultants and agencies managing client relationships
- technical operators who want local control
- open-source contributors who want to improve the product they use

These users often avoid traditional CRMs because they feel heavy, sales-led, and
administrative. They still need account memory, follow-up discipline, and clear
source-backed decisions.

## Product Promise

Help small teams know what changed, who needs attention, and what action is
grounded in actual evidence.

The product can improve with its users. Requests, workflow pain, usage patterns,
and release outcomes may inform future work when they are reviewed and handled
through an appropriate public or private route.

The product must be:

- local-first by default
- useful before integrations are configured
- source-grounded rather than magical
- agent-assisted but human-approved
- open enough to be extended and inspected
- suitable for a hosted edition when teams want convenience

## Core Loops

1. Signals arrive from calls, email, reviews, support, community, GitHub, usage,
   and market research.
2. The CRM links those signals to accounts, ideas, risks, and next actions.
3. Codex or another agent prepares drafts, briefs, specs, and follow-up work
   from explicit context.
4. Humans review and approve work.
5. The Evolution Log records what changed and why.
6. Public-safe feedback can appear in the Open CRM Buildroom.

## Product shape

The product prioritizes daily usefulness:

- account board
- account detail
- signal capture
- signal inbox
- idea loop
- Codex task queue
- export/reset controls
- privacy guardrails
- reviewed public feedback handoff

The differentiator is the loop between source evidence, next action, product
improvement, and agent work. Large enterprise customization and decorative
dashboards are outside the core product focus.
