# Product narrative

Zentrik Open CRM is a useful open-source CRM that helps small teams remember
account context, choose the next action, and keep decisions tied to evidence.
It is designed for founders, consultants, agencies, and technical operators who
want less CRM administration and more clarity about their relationships.

The product also makes a human-and-agent workflow easy to understand:

```text
source evidence → account memory → next action → review → outcome
```

An agent can read an explicit workspace snapshot, prepare a bounded proposal,
and show the source behind it. A person decides what lands, what is shared, and
what happens next.

## Product promise

Open CRM helps a team:

- remember what matters about every account
- see who owns the next action
- connect recommendations to calls, notes, reviews, and other sources
- work locally with a browser, CLI, or MCP client
- review agent proposals before they change records
- export a small, public-safe feedback bundle when it wants to shape the product

The CRM is useful on its own. Zentrik provides an optional path for processing
reviewed product feedback, while local records remain under the user's control.

## Voice

Use direct language:

- built with its users
- source-grounded account work
- human-reviewed agent work
- local first, portable by design
- show why it changed

Avoid claims of unchecked autonomy, automatic sending, guaranteed roadmap
delivery, or replacement of the founder, operator, seller, or product thinker.

## Principles

- **Useful first:** the account workflow must stand on its own.
- **Local first:** a team can begin without connecting a hosted service.
- **Source grounded:** facts, interpretations, and decisions remain distinct.
- **Human approved:** agents prepare work; people retain judgment and authority.
- **Public safe:** private records and credentials stay out of public artifacts.
- **Portable:** records, feedback, and agent handoffs use inspectable formats.
- **Measurable:** releases should name the intended outcome and how it will be
  checked.

## Audience

Open CRM is for founder-led B2B teams, independent builders, consultants,
agencies, technical operators, and contributors who care about data control. It
is not intended to replace an ERP, billing system, or security incident system.

## Product evolution

Users can keep feedback local, prepare a reviewed GitHub issue, or use a verified
hosted route when one is available. A public request is evidence to review, not a
commitment to ship. Releases should explain what changed, why it changed, and
what outcome was checked.
